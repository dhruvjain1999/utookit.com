Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Tookit LLC ("Tookit", "we", "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Tookit you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

To run the reminder service, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Phone number (the number that receives reminders)
  • Email address
  • Reminder times and your timezone
  • An optional nickname and a short personal note, if you choose to add one (used only to personalize your reminder — never medication or health details)
  • Emergency contact name and phone number(s)

What we do not collect

Tookit does not store or disclose any medication names. We store contact information and reminder preferences only. We never see your card details — payments are handled entirely by Stripe.

How we store your information

Your information is stored in secure, access-controlled systems that only Tookit staff can access — including spreadsheet-based records we use to operate the service and keep track of reminder and response history. Payment information is handled entirely by our payment processor, Stripe, and never touches our servers.

If something goes wrong

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. In the unlikely event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify affected users by text or email without undue delay, and will comply with applicable breach-notification laws.

How we share information

We share your information only with the service providers that run Tookit for us: Stripe (payments), Twilio (text messages and calls), Google (secure storage of your sign-up details) and Tally (our sign-up form). They may use it only to provide the Tookit service. We do not share your information with anyone for marketing, and we never sell it.

Text messaging (SMS) privacy

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Your mobile number is used only to send the reminders and alerts you signed up for.

Message frequency varies by your reminder schedule (up to 3 reminder messages or calls per day). Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out at any time.

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. We never sell your personal data.

How long we keep your information

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When you cancel and request deletion, we delete your personal data.

Your rights

You can request deletion of your data at any time by emailing support@utookit.com. You may opt out of automated messages at any time by replying STOP to any text.

Children

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at support@utookit.com.
